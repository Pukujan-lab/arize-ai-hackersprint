Precedent is a reliable pipeline for multi-agent AI. First up: legal research for professionals.
Multi-agent AI is unreliable in production. Agents hallucinate, collide, and can't be audited. And lawyers can't cite what they can't verify.
Ask Precedent a question, and you get a grounded research memo with verified citations, on a live map.
Watch the agents walk the map: a guided tour of every authority, with a spoken transcript.
Under the hood, a planner, retriever, drafter and safeguard claim tasks through ACS. Eighty runs so far, most answers served from cache.
Every reference is checked against retrieved sources, with caps, fallbacks and honest labels.
