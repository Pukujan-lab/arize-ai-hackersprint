Precedent gives cited legal answers to people who can't afford legal research. Everything runs inside one Daytona sandbox. Octo finds the opinion passages, SurrealDB walks the citation graph, Claude Haiku writes a short answer, and a safeguard rejects any authority that wasn't retrieved. ElevenLabs reads it aloud in a hands-free loop, Arize traces every agent step, and ACS coordinates the agents and logs every run. Rate limits and fallbacks keep it safe and cheap.
