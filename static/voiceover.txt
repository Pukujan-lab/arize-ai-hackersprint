Multi-agent AI breaks in production. Agents collide, act on unverified output, touch real systems unsafely, and leave no trace. Precedent is a pipeline that fixes each of these, and every fix is a partner. Daytona isolates the agents in a sandbox. SurrealDB and Octo give them grounded memory. Arize traces and evaluates every step. ElevenLabs gives it a voice. ACS coordinates the agents and audits every run, and a safeguard verifies every citation. Our first use case is verified legal research memos for professionals.
