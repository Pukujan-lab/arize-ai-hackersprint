Live Demo

Ask, then follow up

Can police search my car without a warrant in Colorado?
Yes, with probable cause: the automobile exception allows a warrantless search if police have probable cause the car holds contraband [People v. McMillan; People v. Barton].
↳ follow-up
Can I refuse a search?
You may refuse a consent search, but police with probable cause can search without your permission [1][4]. Refusal does not prevent a lawful warrantless search based on probable cause or other recognized exceptions.
↳ follow-up
What if they find evidence?
Evidence obtained from an unlawful search may be suppressed through a motion to suppress [1][2]. — People v. Warner; People v. Williams
