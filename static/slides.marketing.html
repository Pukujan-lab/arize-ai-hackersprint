The problem

Legal research costs hundreds of dollars a month. Solo lawyers, paralegals and legal aid go without. Generic chatbots invent case law.
03 / 08

Meet Maria

Maria runs a two-lawyer legal-aid office in Pueblo. A client was searched at a traffic stop. Maria has 20 minutes before the hearing and no Westlaw seat. She types one question. Four agents claim their seats. Octo pulls the passages, SurrealDB walks the citation graph across Colorado's courts, Haiku drafts a short answer, and the safeguard refuses to let a single authority through that wasn't actually retrieved. Maria gets three cases, the exact quoted lines, a map of where they were decided — and a disclaimer reminding her to verify. She walks into court prepared.

Safeguards

Every citation must exist in the retrieved set or the run fails. Quotes are verbatim from retrieved passages. "Not legal advice" disclaimer always on. Secrets never printed. Paid model calls cached and minimized.
07 / 08

Why it matters

Grounded, auditable answers; every run logged as an ACS issue record; teammates' agents can claim tasks remotely; works with any dataset, including Hugging Face corpora.
